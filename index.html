Preferred Residential Group Home — Our Mission
Our Mission & Vision

Driven by Purpose, Built for Children

Preferred Residential Group Home was founded with a singular purpose: to create a residential environment where youth experiencing behavioral health challenges can find safety, stability, trauma-informed care, and a genuine path forward.

We believe that with the right structure, compassionate care, life-skills development, and family involvement, every young person has the capacity to grow — and we are committed to building that environment every single day.
