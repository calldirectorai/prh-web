Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I refer a child to Preferred Residential Homes?

You can submit a referral through our online intake form, call us at (623) 385-2064, or email intake@preferredrh.com. We'll respond within 1 business day.

What ages do you serve?

We provide trauma-informed behavioral residential care services for youth. Please contact us directly to confirm current age ranges and placement criteria and availability for specific cases.

What is your current capacity?

We are licensed for up to 5 residents. Availability varies — contact us directly for current placement openings.

Are you licensed by the State of Arizona?

Yes. Preferred Residential Homes is licensed by the Arizona Department of Health Services (ADHS) as a Behavioral Health Residential Facility. License #BHRF2015, effective April 1, 2026, expiring March 31, 2027.

Do you accept DCS / DCFS referrals?

Yes, we work with DCS caseworkers and other referral sources. Please use our intake form or contact us directly to begin the referral process.

What happens after I submit the intake form?

Our team reviews your submission and contacts you within 1 business day. We'll discuss the child's needs, our current availability, and the next steps together.
