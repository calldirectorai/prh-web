Behavioral Residential Services

Structured Residential Care for Behavioral Health

Our core service is providing safe, structured residential placements for youth who require intensive behavioral health support. We create a home-like environment grounded in therapeutic principles, where every child receives consistent, compassionate care around the clock.

  • 24/7 staffed residential environment with trained behavioral health professionals
  • Individualized behavioral support plans developed for each child
  • Regular coordination with caseworkers, therapists, and families
  • Safe, structured routines that promote stability and predictability
  • Licensed by the Arizona Department of Health Services (License #BHRF2015)

We are licensed to provide behavioral health residential facility services in the State of Arizona under Title 36, Chapter 4 of the Arizona Revised Statutes.

Youth Support Programs

Tailored Programs for Children & Adolescents

Every youth in our care participates in structured youth support programming designed to meet them where they are — emotionally, developmentally, and socially. Our programs are adapted to each child's unique needs and build skills that carry forward beyond their time with us.

  • Emotional regulation and coping skills development
  • Social skills training through structured group activities
  • Academic support and homework assistance
  • Positive behavioral reinforcement strategies
  • Age-appropriate recreation, creativity, and physical activity
  • Therapeutic programming in coordination with clinical providers

Family-Centered Care

Keeping Families Connected Every Step of the Way

We believe that family involvement is not optional — it's essential. Even when placement is necessary, maintaining and rebuilding family relationships is a core part of every child's healing journey. We actively partner with families throughout the entire process.

  • Regular family communication and scheduled updates on child's progress
  • Family visitation planning and coordination
  • Parent and guardian involvement in care plan development and reviews
  • Connection to community resources and wraparound support services
  • Transition planning for family reunification when appropriate
  • Culturally sensitive and trauma-informed approach to family engagement

Structured Daily Living Support

Building Independence Through Daily Structure

Structure isn't a restriction — it's a foundation. Our structured daily living program teaches youth the routines, responsibilities, and life-skills they need to function independently and successfully. Every day is intentional.

  • Consistent daily schedules covering morning, afternoon, and evening routines
  • Meal planning, preparation, and nutrition education
  • Household responsibilities appropriate to age and ability
  • Personal hygiene and self-care skill development
  • Money management and community living basics for older youth
  • Time management and organizational skills

Individual Development Planning

A Personalized Roadmap for Every Child

No two youth are the same, and no two development plans should be either. We create individualized development plans in collaboration with the child, their family, and their treatment team — setting realistic, measurable goals and celebrating every milestone.

  • Comprehensive intake assessment to understand each child's strengths and needs
  • Collaborative goal-setting with child, family, and caseworker
  • Academic and vocational goal tracking
  • Social-emotional development benchmarks
  • Regular plan reviews and updates based on progress
  • Documentation and reporting for placement agencies and courts

Safe Residential Placement

A Secure, Stable Home When Children Need It Most

When a youth needs a safe place to land, we are here. Our residential placement service provides an immediate, stable, and secure living environment staffed around the clock by caring professionals trained in behavioral health support.

  • Immediate placement coordination with referral agencies and caseworkers
  • Clean, comfortable, fully furnished private and semi-private rooms
  • Secure, monitored environment with clearly defined safety protocols
  • Trained staff on-site 24 hours a day, 7 days a week
  • Emergency placement options available — contact us for availability
  • Regular communication with placing agencies throughout the placement

Intake & Screening

Our Intake & Screening Process

We've designed our intake process to be as clear, respectful, and efficient as possible — for families, caseworkers, and referral sources alike. Here's what to expect from the moment you reach out.

  • Submit your inquiry online or call us directly at (623) 385-2064
  • A team member reviews your submission within 1 business day
  • We conduct an initial screening call to assess fit and availability
  • Documentation review: referral docs, assessments, and history
  • Placement decision made in coordination with all parties
  • Welcome and transition support provided on arrival
