Our Story

About Preferred Residential Homes for Youth

At Preferred Residential Homes, we are committed to creating a safe, structured, compassionate, and healing environment where youth can feel supported, valued, and empowered to succeed. Our mission is to provide trauma-informed care, emotional support, life-skills development, and behavioral health guidance to help young people build confidence, stability, and hope for their future.

Preferred Residential Homes was created from a passion to support youth who need guidance, structure, emotional support, and a nurturing environment to thrive. We believe every child deserves to feel safe, heard, respected, and supported regardless of their life circumstances.

Our goal is to partner with families, case managers, behavioral health providers, and community agencies to help youth reach their full potential emotionally, socially, academically, and personally. We are licensed by the Arizona Department of Health Services as a Behavioral Health Residential Facility (License #BHRF2015).

Leadership

Dr. Odilia S. Kwateng, DNP, MSN, APRN, FNP-C, PMHNP-BC

Founder & Executive Director — Preferred Residential Homes, LLC
Board-Certified PMHNP & FNP-C · 19+ Years Clinical Experience

Dr. Kwateng is a Board-Certified Psychiatric Mental Health Nurse Practitioner and Family Nurse Practitioner with extensive experience in crisis intervention, behavioral health services, trauma-informed care, medication management, psychotherapy support, and interdisciplinary treatment planning.

She currently serves as a Behavioral Health Mental Nurse Practitioner at Mind 24/7 Crisis Center in Phoenix, Arizona, where she provides psychiatric evaluations, crisis stabilization, suicide risk assessment, and mental health care coordination for youth and families.

Throughout her career, Dr. Kwateng has worked in hospitals, crisis centers, primary care, and mental health settings, caring for diverse populations with compassion, professionalism, and cultural understanding. Her background includes emergency care, intensive care, behavioral health, chronic disease management, psychotherapy support, and family-centered treatment planning.
